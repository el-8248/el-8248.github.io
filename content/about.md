I am a member of the **mechanical department** of [ChainLynx Robotics](https://chainlynx8248.com), A [FRC](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/frc/) competition robotics team based out of Lincoln High School. I am currently in a general role, training new members, coordinating for events, and preparing for the upcoming season. Once the 2026-27 season begins, I will have a more specific role in the robot design and build.

During the 2025-26 season, I was the **lead** for the **chassis subsystem**. During the design phase, I modeled the base platform using CAD *(Computer Aided Design)* software, and collaborated with my other team members to **integrate all of our systems into a high-functioning robot**. After which, I led a team through the **manufacturing and assembly** processes. Here I used knowledge about material behaviours and **collaborated with mentors** to create strong 3d prints, and I used CAM *(Computer Aided Machining)* and my team's CNC router to create structural components out of aluminum and polycarbonate.

Currently the 2026-27 has not begun. I am currently focusing on the **training and development** of our new members, guiding the future of ChainLynx. In parallel I am still doing routine maintenance, preparing for competition and working on my CAD skills to better prepare myself for the upcoming season.
